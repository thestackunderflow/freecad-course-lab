  What I found:

  - Hole wall: this is the wall at y =
    +20…+23. The hole is 5 mm across, centred
    at x = 0, z = 30. It is cut from the outer
    face inward. The wall's outer face moves
    with wall, and the hole's start plane
    followed it correctly. The z = 30 position
    is typed in, not linked to cup_height.
  - Peg wall: this is the opposite wall, at y
    = −20…−23, with no hole in it. The hole
    and the peg are on opposite walls. If you
    meant the peg to be on the same wall as
    the hole, or the hole on the peg's wall,
    that's wrong and I haven't changed it.
  - Peg: it is 10×10 mm, centred at x = 0 and
    z = 40 (z 35–45), and it starts at y =
    −20, the inside face of the wall. That has
    three consequences:
    - The peg tip is always at y = −35, so it
      sticks out only peg_length − wall, which
      is 12 mm at wall 3. Thicker walls
      shorten the peg. If you want 15 mm
      outside the wall, the peg needs to start
      at the outer face or its length needs
      to be peg_length + wall.
    - The peg's z centre uses a typed-in 40 −
      peg_size/2, not cup_height. It won't
      follow height changes.
    - The peg's lower edge is only 2.5 mm
      above the hole's top edge in z (peg
      bottom z = 35, hole top z = 32.5).
      That's fine as they're on different
      walls, but it's tight if you ever move
      them to the same wall.
  - Skeleton: the walls and floor grow
    outward, so the outside is 66×46 and the
    floor is at z = −3…0. The cup_width and
    cup_length values are therefore inner
    dimensions.
