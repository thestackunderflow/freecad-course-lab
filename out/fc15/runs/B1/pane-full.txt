  Why 0.45 and not rounder
  - Limit: the two radii together can't exceed
    the wall, because the rim top is only one
    wall thick.
  - Flat strip: 0.45 each leaves a flat strip
    of about 0.1 × wall (0.2 mm at 2 mm). A
    perfect half-round (0.5 each) would leave
    no flat at all, and that fails in the CAD
    kernel. You can push rim_round a bit
    higher if you want, but I haven't tested
    above 0.45.
  - Your rim_out and rim_in: they're still in
    the sheet, unchanged and unused. 3 mm and
    1 mm add up to more than a 2 mm wall, so
    they can't be applied as they stand.
