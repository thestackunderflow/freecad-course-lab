
  - Features added: RimInner and RimOuter
    fillets sit at the end of the Cup body
    (inner first, then outer).
  - Driven by wall: both radii now come from
    the rim_in and rim_out cells in Params. I
    changed those cells to =wall * 0.4, so the
    radius scales with the wall (0.8 mm at
    the current 2 mm wall).
  - Why 0.4, not fully round: a perfectly
    round rim would need a radius of 0.5 ×
    wall on each side, which leaves no flat
    top. FreeCAD rejects that, and fractions
    of 0.42 or more failed at every wall I
    tried. 0.4 is the roundest setting that
    held up, and it leaves a thin flat of
    about 20% of the wall.
  - Old rim values: rim_out was 3 and rim_in
    was 1, and nothing used them. A 3 mm
    radius can't fit on a 2 mm wall, so I
    replaced them. If you want different
    values, edit those two cells, but keep
    each one under about 0.4 × wall.
  - Wall direction: the wall grows outward
    from the original 60×40 sketch, so the
    inside stays 60×40 and the outside is
    64×44. That was already how the model
    worked, and I left it alone.
