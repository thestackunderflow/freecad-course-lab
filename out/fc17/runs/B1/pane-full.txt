● I fixed it. The mirrored peg now sits on the
  front next to the original, and nothing
  sticks out of the back.

  The fix: the Mirrored feature was mirroring
  across the XZ plane, which flips front to
  back. I changed MirrorPlane to the YZ plane,
  which flips left to right. It's driven by
  Params.peg_x, so it follows the peg when you
  move it.

  What I found:
  - Pegs: both are on the front face at the
    same height (z 35–45). They're 10×10 mm
    and stick out 15 mm. The original is at x
    = +10 to +20 and the copy at x = −20 to
    −10, so each is 15 mm from the middle. The
    model is valid and still one solid.
  - Moving the peg: I set peg_x to 25 and both
    pegs moved out to ±25, with nothing left
    at ±15. I then put it back to 15, so your
    file is unchanged apart from the mirror
    fix.
  - Back of the cup: nothing sticks out past
    the back wall. The overall extents are now
    y −35 to +22 (they were −35 to +35 with
    the stray peg). The back wall is solid
    except for the 5 mm hanging hole near z ≈
    30 (centered on x = 0, as far as I could
    tell).

  Things to know:
  - The pocket for the hole isn't part of the
    mirror. That's fine for a single hole, but
    if you want a hole through the second peg
    too, it needs mirroring as well.
  - The peg's height is hard-coded in the
    sketch (40 - peg_size/2) rather than
    coming from Params. Say so if you want it
    moved into the spreadsheet.
