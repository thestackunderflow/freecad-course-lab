❯ My wall cup should be 60 by 40 inside, but
  after the shell it came out smaller. Fix it

● The sketch is the outer profile, and the
  Thickness feature cuts inward. So the inside
  comes out as 60−2·wall by 40−2·wall. I'll
  make the sketch size the outside as inside +
  2·wall.

  Called freecad (ctrl+o to expand)

● The inside of the cup now stays 60 × 40
  whatever the wall thickness. I checked this
  by slicing the model at mid-height at three
  wall thicknesses:

  ┌──────┬─────────┬─────────┐
  │ Wall │ Inside  │ Outside │
  ├──────┼─────────┼─────────┤
  │ 2    │ 60 × 40 │ 64 × 44 │
  ├──────┼─────────┼─────────┤
  │ 3    │ 60 × 40 │ 66 × 46 │
  ├──────┼─────────┼─────────┤
  │ 1.5  │ 60 × 40 │ 63 × 43 │
  └──────┴─────────┴─────────┘

  Cause: The sketch was the outer profile, and
  the Thickness feature cuts inward from it.
  So the inside was always 60 − 2·wall by 40 −
  2·wall.

  Fix: I changed the two sketch constraints to
  read as follows:
  - cup_width is now Params.cup_width + 2 *
    Params.wall.
  - cup_length is now Params.cup_length + 2 *
    Params.wall.

  cup_width and cup_length in the Params sheet
  now mean the inside size. I put wall back
  to 2 after testing.

  Height: cup_height is still the overall
  outside height, because the pad is
  unchanged. The floor is wall thick, so the
  inside depth is cup_height − wall. If you
  want cup_height to be the inside depth
  instead, change the pad length to
  Params.cup_height + Params.wall.
