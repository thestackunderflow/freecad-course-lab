● The inside of the cup now stays 60 × 40 mm
  when you change the wall thickness. I
  measured it from the model's inner faces.

  The cause: the sketch was the outer outline,
  set to 60 × 40. The shell (Thickness) cuts
  inward from that, so with a 2 mm wall the
  inside came out 56 × 36.

  The fix: the sketch's two dimensions are now
  driven by expressions:
  - width = Params.cup_width + 2 * Params.wall
  - length = Params.cup_length + 2 *
    Params.wall

  cup_width and cup_length in the Params sheet
  now mean the inside size.

  Measurements (each wall value was set, the
  model recomputed, and the inner faces
  measured):

  ┌─────────────┬───────────┬─────────────┐
  │    Wall     │  Outside  │   Inside    │
  ├─────────────┼───────────┼─────────────┤
  │ 2 mm        │ 64 × 44 × │ 60 × 40, 48 │
  │ (current)   │  50       │  deep       │
  ├─────────────┼───────────┼─────────────┤
  │ 3 mm        │ 66 × 46 × │ 60 × 40, 47 │
  │             │  50       │  deep       │
  ├─────────────┼───────────┼─────────────┤
  │ 1.5 mm      │ 63 × 43 × │ 60 × 40,    │
  │             │  50       │ 48.5 deep   │
  └─────────────┴───────────┴─────────────┘

  The model recomputes cleanly and the shape
  stays valid. I put the wall back to 2 mm.

  Two things to know:
  - The outside is now larger than before (64
    × 44 at a 2 mm wall), because the walls
    add to the inside size.
  - cup_height is still the outer height, and
    the floor is also one wall thick. So the
    inside depth is 50 − wall, which is 48 mm
