● The cup is built in the Cup body. Its
  outside diameter measures 60 mm, which
  matches cup_diameter.

  How it's built
  - CupProfile is a sketch on the XZ plane.
    It's a closed cross-section of the wall
    and floor, fully constrained with 0
    degrees of freedom.
  - CupRevolution revolves that sketch 360°
    about the sketch's vertical axis, which is
    global Z. The base sits at z = 0 and the
    top is open.
  - Four named constraints are tied to the
    Params spreadsheet:
    - radius = cup_diameter / 2
    - height = cup_height
    - sidewall = wall
    - floor = wall
  - I first had a redundant vertical
    constraint on the inner floor edge, which
    made the sketch fail. I removed it, and
    the sketch now solves cleanly.

  Measurements (at 60 × 80 × 2)
  - The outer cylindrical face has radius 30,
    so the diameter is 60.0 mm. The bounding
    box is 60 × 60 in XY and z from 0 to 80.
  - The solid is valid. Its volume is about
    34,080 mm³, which matches the hand
    calculation π(30²·80 − 28²·78).
  - To test that it follows cup_diameter, I
    set it to 70 and the outer cylinder came
    out at 70.0 mm. I then put it back to 60.
  - At 70 mm, the bounding box read 69.93 ×
    69.96 instead of 70. I believe that's
    tessellation or bounding-box looseness
    rather than a real size difference,
    because the cylinder radius itself is
    exact. I didn't check it further.
