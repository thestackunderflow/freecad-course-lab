  Thickness shell. The shell hollowed it
  along with the cup and grew it outward by
  the wall thickness, so it came out hollow
  and oversized, and open into the cup.

  Fix:
  - Order: the feature order is now Pad (box),
    then Thickness (shell), then Pad001
    (peg), and the peg is the body's Tip. The
    shell no longer touches the peg.
  - Open face: the shell's open face was
    recorded as Pad:Face4, which is the −X
    side wall, but it was actually opening the
    top. I pointed it at the top face
    (Pad:Face6) so it can't drift.

  = −20, which is the cavity side of the wall,
  because the shell grows outward. It reaches
  y = −35 whatever the wall is, so the part
  sticking out is peg_length − wall, about 13
  mm at the default wall. Before, the tip sat
  at about −37 mm, so it now sticks out about
  2 mm less.
